Testing, as you asked. Both new codes resolve to the right places: the LWC code goes to the "Ebenezer Little White Church" giving page and the PC code to the "Ebenezer PC" page. I walked both pages in a browser and confirmed the fund dropdowns are correct, including that the Beat the Drum fund still appears on the LWC page (our missions pages tell donors to select it there).

One concern with the new images, and a fix. Taking you up on your offer to adjust the design: the labels printed across the middle of the codes cover part of the pattern, and the yellow one is low contrast, so scanners have less margin than usual, especially in print or dim light. When I ran the images through three different QR scanner programs, the yellow LWC code passed only one of the three and the PC code passed none, though phone cameras will often still manage. Attached are cleaned-up versions of both (previews below): same links, same labels, but with the text moved below the code and the pattern in high-contrast dark on white. These pass all three scanners. Feel free to use them for print if you like them, or I can adjust colors and wording however you want.

On the website itself, I kept a single giving path pointing at the LWC page, same as before, and did not add the PC donation code anywhere, consistent with the direction from July to keep Pardue material off the site. If you'd like the PC giving link on the website somewhere, that's a quick change; just say the word.

It would still be worth a couple of people scanning the codes with their actual phones (the ones in this email or the attachments work for that), since a phone camera is the real test. Mine scanned fine here.
