Women's Retreat

October 30 - November 1, 2026 · Woodlands Camp

Ladies, join us for a weekend away: a time to rest, laugh, connect with one another, and grow deeper in your walk with God. The retreat is open to all Ebenezer women. Whether you've been part of Ebenezer for decades or just walked in the door, there's a place for you.

  • WhereWoodlands Camp, Cleveland, GA
  • WhenFriday, October 30 (5:00 PM) through Sunday morning, November 1, 2026
  • Cost$50 nonrefundable registration fee. The remainder of the costs have been generously provided by a donor. If you're unable to attend, your fee will be applied to the Women's Ministry toward future events.
  • LodgingDouble rooms only. Name a preferred roommate when you register, or we'll be glad to assign one for you.
  • QuestionsContact Kim Hays: text 678-662-3320 or email kimhays3@yahoo.com
